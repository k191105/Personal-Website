01 / LANGUAGE
LANGUAGE MODELLING · PYTORCH Built from scratch

A language model with a personal reading list

I built a small language model to capture something of my intellectual profile, training it on thinkers and writers I enjoy: Russell, Bourdieu, Hofstadter, and Wilde, alongside my own essays. It’s a 25-million-parameter Llama-style model, built from scratch in PyTorch and trained on 1,941 books and essays.

Chat with the model — coming soon.

Read project notes +

Inside the implementation

The model brings together byte-pair encoding, rotary position embeddings, grouped-query attention, RMSNorm, SwiGLU, and AdamW. Training used 212 million tokens drawn from books and essays.

From text to transformer

This project follows the language-model pipeline all the way from the choice of text to the mechanics of a transformer. Its scale makes the components tangible: the tokenizer, architecture, and training corpus are parts of the same system.

02 / BUILDING
BITEWISE LABS · SWIFTUI & METAL Three shipped apps

A little more room for culture

I founded Bitewise Labs and built Literature Bites, Art Bites, and Architecture Bites: a suite of iOS apps for cultural education. Alongside the apps, I built automated video and 3D monument-rendering pipelines.

From content to a working product

The suite brings literature, art, and architecture into three focused apps. The engineering spans the native SwiftUI experience, Metal, and the tools that produce visual content for the apps.

Bringing the pieces together

The three released apps bring together native software and educational content. The automated pipelines support the ongoing work of creating videos and rendering architectural models.
